Amazing as it is, the personal computer is not a perfected thing.
It's a wobbly tower made up of decades of decisions, all built upon a single assumption: that computers can't understand humans.
But now, they can.
The Jenga tower of computing is already toppling. Personal computing is about to be reinvented. Again.

We see that the true power of AI isn't in mindless automation and generative slop, but rather in its ability to understand users and their intent. This creates the opportunity for a computing revolution even greater and more exciting than the shift from CLI to GUI forty years ago.

Telepath is building the software stack for this new personal computing experience, from first principles. We call it a sensemaking computer. It understands you as a unique user, and it uses that understanding to help you make sense of the flood of information that defines modern life. We believe this will result in a new kind of computer that is more powerful, useful, and joyful than anything that's come before. You can get a sneak peek of our thinking and plans here.
